Works without internet

Pages, face models and proctoring are all served from the exam server in your building.

Faculty decide

The AI invigilator only flags moments. A teacher reviews each one and accepts or invalidates the attempt.

No video recording

Only still images of flagged moments are kept, for faculty to review, and they are deleted {{ evidence_retention_days() }} days after results are published.

How an exam runs

  1. 1

    Set the paper

    Upload questions from CSV, Excel or JSON, split a bank into sets, choose the window, the proctoring and who may sit it.

  2. 2

    Sit it in the hall

    Students check their camera and identity, enter the access key, and answer in fullscreen. Answers save as they go.

  3. 3

    Review and release

    Faculty watch attempts live, review flagged ones, then publish results, answer reviews and PDF reports.

For faculty

  • Live attempts, extra time and ending an exam from one page
  • Evidence review before any result is released
  • Batch reports, leaderboards and CSV/PDF exports

For students

  • A calm, distraction-free exam page
  • Answers saved automatically, even if the browser closes
  • Your result and a question-by-question review once released
